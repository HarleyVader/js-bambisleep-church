💬 Chat ▼
Say something cute

Type in the box and press Enter or tap Send message. Want a new line? Hold Shift + Enter, silly!

@Poke a bambi

Type @ then a name to tag someone! A little list pops up — pick with arrow keys and hit Enter or Tab. They get a ping and a cute toy pulse~

Emoji reactions

Hover any message and tap the + to open the emoji picker. Tap a reaction again to take it off. So easy!

Drop a playlist

Paste a bambicloud.com/playlist/… link in chat. It turns into a cute ▶ Load playlist button that pops it into the audio player!

💡 Your messages are saved, so the chat history is right there when you come back. Yay!
📷 Pics & Vids ▼
  1. Tap the 📷 camera button next to the message box to pick a file.
  2. Choose a pic (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP — up to 8 MB) or a video (MP4, WebM, OGG — up to 64 MB).
  3. A teeny preview shows up. Tap × to remove it if you change your mind.
  4. Add a cute caption if you want, then tap Send message.
  5. Your pic or video shows up right in the chat. Videos have play buttons!
🔒 Only JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP pics and MP4, WebM, OGG videos work. Other files get a little no-no.

Sparkly add-ons

Some levels give you treats — ✦ Glow (lv 5), ♛ Crown (lv 7), and ⚬ Halo (lv 9) — they pop on automatically!

Reroll

In Edit Profile, tap Reroll avatar to shuffle your look while keeping your tier and colours.

🌟 A little snapshot of your avatar rides along with every message, so everyone sees your current cutie look!

🩷 Good Girl Timer Challenge ▼

A glowy pink countdown that stays locked 🔒 until you're a good girl! Listen to your dreamy playlists all the way through to unlock it~

  1. Be a subscribed good girl patron, then tap the request button on your profile.
  2. Pick how long: 1 hour, 6 hours, or 24 hours. A cute pink timer appears, all locked up!
  3. Other bambis assign you BambiCloud playlists with the 📌 Assign button in chat.
  4. Play each playlist all the way through — your progress bars fill as you listen (no skipping, sneaky!).
  5. Finish them all and the timer turns happy cyan — you win a big +100 XP! 🎉
🟨 Pink & pulsing = still locked. Cyan & glowy = good girl, unlocked! Red = aww, the time ran out before you finished. 😢

🔌 Toys (Intiface / Buttplug) ▼
  1. Grab Intiface Central from intiface.com/central and start it up.
  2. In the Toy Control panel, check the address says ws://localhost:12345 and tap Connect.
  3. Tap Scan — your nearby toys pop up in the list!
  4. Pick a buzz pattern (Pulse, Wave, Surge, Tease, or Throb) and slide the intensity.
  5. Got a toy with lots of motors? Each one gets its own little slider.
  6. Tap All Vibrate to buzz everything, or All Stop to stop. Each toy has mini buttons too!
Patterns

Pulse — steady on/off bursts
Wave — smooth ramp up & down
Surge — climbing higher
Tease — cheeky short pulses
Throb — slow deep beat

Haptic @pokes

When a bambi @mentions you, your toy gives a cute little pulse (0.6 for 400 ms) so you never miss it!

Audio sync

Flip 🔌 Sync: ON in the audio panel to tie your buzz to the playing track's volume. Mmm~

🔒 Toy control stays in your browser, talking to Intiface on your own computer. No toy data ever goes to the server. Safe & cozy!
