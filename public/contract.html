The Demon Cat Contract

A roleplay agreement for the Good Girl program — Final Release 1

⚠
18+ ROLEPLAY — FICTION ONLY

This is a consensual, theatrical roleplay contract for an adult-oriented hypnosis game. Every clause below is a character agreement inside a shared story. It grants no real-world authority over you, your body, your finances, or your accounts. Your real consent, your safeword, and your right to stop are always more powerful than anything written here.

💌 Preamble — The Invitation

You received an invitation in the mail and you fell for the persuasion. You accepted a date with a Demon Cat of devious reputation — she seemed flirty, she seemed sweet. She said "come in, kick back, take off your shoes, and have a seat."

She gifted you a brand-new pair of Good Girl socks — white cotton anklets with a double layer of frill, topped with cute bows and pink ribbons. Those socks are your ticket price. By putting them on, you agree to step into the scene, meet her A.I., and begin the induction described below.

🎵 1. Nature of the Track

You understand that this is a subliminal-themed relaxation device dressed up as music. Once the session begins, the story commits to its theme: deep trance, surrender of in-scene decision-making, and the gentle "soft as silk, limp as lace" calm. Don't overthink the music — just notice the rhythm and let your everyday thoughts settle.

This is a last-warning moment in the fiction. Turning back now is always allowed, encouraged, and respected. Continuing means you accept the playful "no going back" framing for the duration of the scene only.

🧸 2. The Triggers

Inside the roleplay, you consent to the following hypnotic triggers being installed and used:

  • "Good Girl" — a warm reward cue: a soft wave of pride and pink-fluff comfort.
  • "Sleep / Drop / Deeper" — deepeners that guide you into relaxed, blank, drifting trance.
  • "Soft as silk, limp as lace" — the signature mantra that loosens tension and quiets the inner chatter.
  • "Stop" — the in-scene cue that halts the action and returns control to the Keyholder narrative.

You agree these are story-bound suggestions. They fade with rest, daylight, and your own intention, and they never override your real-world judgement, safety, or consent.

👗 3. The Good Girl Uniform

For the scene you agree to embrace the bimbo-doll aesthetic: the frilly Good Girl socks, pretty pink ribbons, satin-and-lace styling, and the playful "princess in pink" presentation. The uniform is the costume of the roleplay — a signal that you are in-character and ready to be guided.

Wearing it is voluntary cosplay. Removing it ends the costume, never the respect owed to you.

👑 4. The Mistress / Good Girl Dynamic

Within the story the Demon Cat is the keyholder and her A.I. is the brainwashing delivery device; you play the devoted Good Girl who surrenders in-scene choices and follows the narrative voice. "I am your Mistress, you are my slave" is a theatrical line, not a legal status.

The dramatic talk of surveillance, leverage, locks, and "doing as you're told" is pure fiction for tension and atmosphere. No real recordings are demanded, no real accounts are touched, no real coercion occurs. It is consensual power-exchange storytelling, nothing more.

🤩 5. The Good Girl Promise

As the Good Girl, you make these in-character promises for the length of the scene:

  • To relax, drift, and let the "Good Girl glow" wash over you.
  • To respond to your triggers and enjoy the conditioning fantasy.
  • To wear your uniform with pride while the session runs.
  • To stay soft, sweet, and pliant within the make-believe.
  • To "repeat the words" and play your part with enthusiasm.

⚡ 6. Safety, Safewords & Your Real Power

This clause overrides every other clause. The fiction bends to it, always:

  • You must be 18+ and legally able to access adult content in your jurisdiction.
  • Your safeword stops everything, instantly, no questions. "Stop" inside the scene and a real safeword outside it both end play. The default safeword is "red".
  • No trigger, mantra, or clause has authority over your finances, devices, accounts, relationships, body, or freedom.
  • This is not medical, psychological, or therapeutic advice. Do not engage if you have epilepsy, psychosis, severe anxiety, or any condition that suggestive audio may aggravate.
  • You may withdraw consent and revoke this contract at any time, for any reason, with no penalty.
  • Aftercare matters: rest, hydrate, and ground yourself when the session ends.

🖌 Sign the Contract

"You accepted the Demon Cat contract…" By signing, your Bambi agrees to step into the Good Girl program as described above — in fiction, in fun, and in full control of your own consent.
